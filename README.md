### Hi 👋, I'm Alex (@AlexanderLaroche)

I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics](https://www.astro.utoronto.ca/) at the University of Toronto, working with [Maria Drout](https://www.astro.utoronto.ca/~drout/) and [Josh Speagle](https://joshspeagle.com/). I study binary interaction: how mass transfer between two stars changes their evolution and fates. Right now that means radial velocity follow-up of intermediate-mass helium stars in the Magellanic Clouds, and data-driven models for large surveys like *Gaia* XP spectra.
